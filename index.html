Connection settings

Leave blank for this browser’s local demo mode. For multi-device sync, configure a Gun peer you control and use the same URL on each device.

Privacy & security

GunDB is a synchronization database, not a guarantee of end-to-end privacy. A peer/relay may receive replicated data. This prototype uses demo auth and does not yet encrypt posts or messages end-to-end. Do not use it for sensitive information.
